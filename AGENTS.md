# User interface preferences

These are explicit preferences from the project owner. Preserve them in future UI work:

- Keep the Starhull font. Render bitmap text at crisp pixel sizes; fix layout and scaling rather than substituting a different body font.
- Avoid hover tooltips. The conditional lock explanation over Ship Designer is the exception the user accepts. Show necessary prices, restrictions and feedback inline or in a panel opened by clicking.
- Keep the three general Fleet design slots visible without scrolling at supported sizes, including 640×360. No dedicated Science/Civic slots, singleton support rules, offense limits or equipment segregation; players choose their own combinations within normal research and deployment capacity rules.
- No Port or Story buttons, service-explanation prose or automatic story popups in the game UI. Preserve underlying arrival/repair/research/story progression. The top bar shows resources and level, without Act/row or duplicate ship-count/hull/damage statistics; fleet condition belongs in the fleet summary.
- Put actions for an individual physical ship beside that ship in the formation view, only while exactly one ship is selected. Do not repeat Reserve/Deploy buttons for every ship in the fleet summary.
- No Tab or arrow-key cycling between UI controls, and no focus-outline boxes on buttons, tabs, sliders or texture controls. Text fields still accept typing and canvas/gameplay shortcuts remain available.
- UI fonts and reusable component styles belong in `themes/ui_theme.tres`; use theme inheritance/type variations instead of per-control font resources, fallback fonts or copied style boxes. Changing the theme's default font should update the UI.
- Reserved ships are absent from formation rendering and hit testing. Manage them in a small modal opened by a Reserve button.
- Shop slots have no minus/salvage button. Salvage the singly selected physical hull through its formation actions immediately, without confirmation. Construction opens a plural quantity-order window: preview/base sprite costs/equipment on the left, actual order breakdown on the right, shared top-right X and centered Construct button; no large Cancel button.
- Do not add unrequested features while implementing remaining milestones. Ask before adding a feature outside the requested scope.
- Do not offer individual construction-material purchases. Construction/repair and other material-consuming actions use owned materials first, then charge credits for shortages at the existing material exchange rates. Credits act as material wild cards; this is separate from the planned cargo-goods trade.

- Research is one pannable/zoomable tree with categories as major branches, never category filters. Omit Foundation entirely from the UI; its knowledge is assumed. Keep research text minimal: names, unlocks, essential numbers and Set Focus. Completed nodes show a drawn green check above the name; partial nodes show current / required RP above the name. The left panel shows production and planned arrival yield, not categories. Each living deployed lab produces 1 RP/day, plus a fleet baseline of 0.5 RP/day (including no labs); multiply by planned travel days, typically 2-4 per route. Preserve fractional RP, focus and partial projects across saves. Freeze journey time/labs at departure and settle once on completed travel; battle duration never farms RP. Only the header shows the unspent RP balance. No View Grants, node modals, bad disclosure glyphs, next labels or instant-purchase UI.

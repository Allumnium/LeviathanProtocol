# Current game: physical hangar

The actual game uses scripts/hangar_state.gd (autoload GameState), scenes/game_map.tscn and scripts/hangar_screen.gd. The owner replaced materials, recipes and ship design with physical chassis and equipment instances. No designer, blueprints, material inventory, construction grids, equipment orientation, reserves, design-family limits or deployment-capacity system in the active game. Weapons aim automatically and thrust is ship-wide.

Ships own fixed typed mounts. Offense/Defense/Tech/Civic and S/M/L/XL require EXACT type AND size matches. Shared equipment inventory contains unique IDs and persistent damage. Fitting moves an instance for free; removing it returns it to stock. No free copies. Buy researched equipment and chassis only at shipyards; battles also award physical equipment loot. Credits are the sole spendable resource. Passenger/cargo trade and lab/travel research are explicitly retained.

Use assets/flagship_basic.png for the mandatory starting flagship. Exactly one flagship deploys with every battle. It supplies command points; other owned ships consume chassis command costs. Its loss ends the run even if escorts survive. Current draft budget is 12. Other supplied chassis: Corsair, Fighter, Transport, Frigate and Drone. Preserve original artwork. Maps, costs and initial tuning live in data/slot_chassis.json and data/slot_equipment.json; they remain drafts.

Chassis integrity is one pool: alive at zero, destroyed strictly below zero. Shields absorb damage first. Equipment hits split half to component / half to chassis; bare hull takes full damage. Fixed command-center loss kills the ship. Built-in engines can be disabled/repaired. Damaged/destroyed equipment remains installed until repaired or moved. Destroyed ships cannot be repaired. Repairs and salvaging use credits. Salvage is immediate without confirmation; the flagship cannot be salvaged. Selected formation ships have small nearby actions.

Defer all slot bonuses and old adjacency-dependent buffs. Do not invent replacements. HangarResearch excludes extraction, undefined slot-bonus equipment and unimplemented research from the actual tree/shop. Gravitic Snare / Disruption II are deferred until the credits-only ability path works: no new purchases, fitting, loot or focus; preserve already-earned research and old item instances so they can be inspected/removed/sold. Existing working fleet-support equipment remains available. Core intermediates now include ship-wide Ammunition Magazines and Tactical Computers: support ALL host weapons, no adjacency or target-count limit, and multiple copies STACK (additive +20% cycling per magazine; synchronization rate adds per computer). Railgun, finite Flight Deck/Bomber Bay and automatic Command Uplink are active. Carrier allowances follow equipment UIDs across retries/transfers; source loss ends sorties. ECM/Phase remain unfinished. See docs/INTERMEDIATE_EQUIPMENT.md. Starting tech is Foundation, hidden from the tree. Equipment research gates purchases and fitting.

# Trade and research

Passenger/cargo loads follow equipment instance IDs, never mount indices. Holds carry 10 goods; cabins carry 4 passengers. Cargo loads when leaving an inhabited port; cabins board for inhabited destinations. Delivery pays credits automatically at inhabited arrivals. No Port/Story buttons or load-management modals. Occupied items must deliver before being unequipped. Destroyed hosts/components lose their loads; passenger casualty fees apply once and may leave credit debt. Repairs never recreate lost loads.

Research is one pannable tree with major branches and minimal inline details/Set Focus. No category filters, Foundation node, View Grants modal, disclosure glyphs or next labels. Completed nodes have a green check above the name; partial nodes show X / Y. Only the header shows unspent RP. Lab I / II / III produce 1 / 2 / 3 RP/day respectively, plus 0.5 fleet baseline. Multiply by travel days (typically 2-4), freeze at departure, retain fractions and settle once on arrival. Battle duration never generates RP. Research progress is not shop currency.

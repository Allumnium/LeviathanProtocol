# Individual ship experience and complete loadouts

Draft for gameplay review, 2026-11-05. Player refitting offers complete loadouts only, with read-only equipment inspection. The 36-preset hardware catalog is implemented by the approved [Act I expansion](ACT_I_EXPANSION_PLAN.md); XP, chevron UI and the level gates below remain proposals.

## Confirmed direction

- Experience belongs to each ship. Reassigning, splitting or merging squadrons moves ships with their existing XP; it does not copy, average away or redistribute that XP.
- Players choose complete named loadouts. Remove part-by-part fitting/removal and custom equipment settings from the player flow. Equipment/mount data remains internal for authored loadouts, combat stats, source artwork and physical load ownership.
- Experience requirements belong to loadouts, not individual equipment. Existing research upgrades and the rare Beam Lance research requirement remain separate conditions.
- Cap experience levels at five. Show compact chevron rank badges inspired by the supplied reference, without copying its military rank names. Most ships have one or two level-1 choices and at most four total choices by level 5. Later presets must offer a meaningful strategic role or demonstrable power increase.

## Proposed first pass: five levels

| Level | Cumulative XP | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 0 | One or two complete starter fits per chassis |
| 2 | 40 | Early specialization, where the chassis has an unlock |
| 3 | 100 | Stronger weapons or productive support fits |
| 4 | 180 | Advanced support or missile specialization |
| 5 | 300 | Veteran fit, reaching four total choices per chassis |

All new ships, including the starting fleet, begin at zero XP. Each chassis keeps its current first/default loadout available at level 1, preserving legal starting fits and fitted purchase prices. Experience only unlocks loadouts; it does not secretly increase damage, hull, speed or research output. Thresholds and the mapping below are proposals, not approved balance. Some ranks do not add another preset because the total is capped at four; no extra hidden stat bonus is implied.

## Chevron presentation

[Rank preview](SHIP_RANK_PREVIEW.svg): one through five stacked gold chevrons, with consistent width and spacing. Use code-native drawing for the actual small UI badge so it remains sharp at supported resolutions; no new bitmap artwork is required. Level numbers and an XP meter belong in ship details, while Fleet rows can use the compact badge. Keep mixed-group rank averages informational, never a fitting requirement. Battle badges should respect visibility and remain small enough to preserve the thin health bars; verify actual overview readability before deciding whether to show all badges or only selected/closely viewed ships.

Battle awards, per surviving deployed ship:

| Contribution | Proposed XP | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participation | `10 * min(combat_seconds / 20, 1)` | 10 per encounter |
| Effective damage dealt to hostile ships | 1 per 10 damage | 15 per encounter |
| Effective hostile ship damage received, hull plus shields | 1 per 20 damage | 5 per encounter |

Count actual absorbed/dealt damage, excluding overkill, asteroids, friendly/self damage, repair/refit changes and generated shield capacity. Keep fractional XP. Participation supports noncombat roles; damage dealt carries more weight than damage taken. Full participation plus small damage contributions should put ordinary ships near level 2 after roughly three to four battles; this is an estimate to verify, not measured campaign pacing.

Award on settlement for victories and withdrawals, prorating participation in short encounters. Do not award on entering deployment or grant repeat rewards for reloading a pending battle. Destroyed ships do not transfer their experience to survivors. Use the existing exactly-once battle receipt and atomic save transaction; save failure must award nothing and retry must award once. The 30-XP combat ceiling limits long healing/shield-recharge loops without changing their gameplay effects.

Income awards: propose 1 XP per 10 credits actually generated by a ship's completed cargo/passenger delivery. Attribute the existing payout inside the ship/equipment loop in [hangar_trade.gd](../scripts/hangar_trade.gd), without changing prices or recreating loads. Battle reward credits, purchases, refunds and salvage do not count as ship-generated income. For the fleet-wide Civil Hospitality income, propose sharing its XP equally among living ships carrying occupied passenger cabins; if none qualify, award no ship XP for that fleet-wide payout. This allocation is also a review decision. Retain fractional income XP, and commit it with the same income/delivery transaction. Paused/inactive Map cannot generate either income or XP.

Science and healing are not additional XP sources in this first proposal. Research ships and healers still earn participation; consider explicit productive-support awards if focused playtesting shows that they fall behind.

## Squadrons, recruits and fitting

Each individual ship's level determines its fitting eligibility. A squadron's displayed average is informational and must never let rookies equip veteran presets or strip existing veteran fits. Proposed row display: floor of the average member level; show the actual level range and number eligible when selecting a loadout for a mixed group.

New purchases use an eligible level-1 preset. Adding a fresh ship to a veteran squadron uses its chassis's level-1 default and marks it as a preset override of the squadron default. Do not charge for or install locked equipment on the recruit. Existing veterans retain their presets. No automatic upgrade when the rookie levels up: a normal refit at an actual shipyard remains necessary.

For a group refit, propose retaining the existing atomic all-or-nothing operation for the ships being changed: every affected member must qualify. Preserve explicitly excluded individual preset overrides. Show a concise requirement such as `Requires all selected ships at level 2`; individual veterans can still change their own preset. No silent partial refit.

The ship inspection view retains hull/shields/stats, cargo/passenger details, repair, salvage and membership controls, plus a complete-loadout selector and a small level/XP meter. Remove equipment swap/removal controls. Clicking a mount may expose read-only equipment information, but never opens a custom fitting flow. Refits retain existing equipment UIDs where applicable and occupied-load guards; XP never recreates hull, cargo or passengers.

## Implementation outline after gameplay approval

1. Persist nonnegative fractional XP on each ship and derive level centrally. Include it in new purchases, checkpoint validation, cloning/restore and all group membership operations.
2. Add hostile-combat contribution accounting alongside existing per-combatant damage totals in [battle_simulation.gd](../scripts/battle_simulation.gd). Current totals can include nonship damage, so do not use them blindly for awards. Stage XP with battle receipts in [hangar_state.gd](../scripts/hangar_state.gd).
3. Attribute delivery/income XP by ship in the existing atomic travel/delivery paths. Ensure frame partitioning, saves, retries and Continue cannot duplicate it.
4. Add `minimum_level` metadata to presets and enforce it centrally for individual/group fitting and fresh purchases. Ensure every chassis has a legal level-1 purchase path. Keep research eligibility independent.
5. Extend the now loadout-only controls in [hangar_screen.gd](../scripts/hangar_screen.gd) with rank/XP and minimum-level feedback. Preserve the existing Fleet inspection, shared X, pan/zoom and shipyard restrictions. Keep concise names, level requirements, prices and errors visible without hover tooltips.
6. Focused checks: XP arithmetic/caps, hostile attribution, partial participation/withdrawal, income attribution, atomic failure/retry, Continue, destroyed/zero-hull boundaries, assignment/split/join, rookies, preset-only fitting restrictions and native Fleet/report feedback. No broad baseline or campaign sweep is implied.

Review needed before XP implementation: approve/change the XP rates and five thresholds, minimum-level mapping and mixed-veteran/recruit rules. Five levels, chevron badges, no custom loadouts and the one/two-to-four option progression are confirmed. Hardware is implemented; XP balance and eligibility rules remain proposals.

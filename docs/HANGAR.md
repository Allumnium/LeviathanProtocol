# Current game

The active target is the existing Act I vertical slice plus remaining D4/D5 in [TODO.md](../TODO.md), not the archived full continuous-world expansion. Preserve implemented systems, saves and source artwork; no materials, construction/designer, equipment stock or component health.

- Entry: [main_menu.tscn](../scenes/main_menu.tscn). GameState: [hangar_state.gd](../scripts/hangar_state.gd). Management: [game_map.tscn](../scenes/game_map.tscn) / [hangar_screen.gd](../scripts/hangar_screen.gd).

- Failed transactions must not change live state or emit success feedback. Surface an explicit retryable save error; preserve backup/recovery behavior.
- Continue preserves earned research, pending upgrade cards in their exact order, banked RP, branch choices, loadouts, ownership and formation. Do not invent save conversions, refit old ships automatically or rewrite old material/blueprint files. Read-only compatibility boundaries are in [LEGACY_BOUNDARIES.md](LEGACY_BOUNDARIES.md).

Locations/events: [expedition_locations.json](../data/expedition_locations.json), [expedition_events.json](../data/expedition_events.json).
- Map is home; Fleet shows the saved battle formation and squadron management. Click a ship for individual settings in the right panel, or its numbered flag for squadron settings. There is no separate Ships tab. Research opens from the header. Switching to Fleet suspends travel while preserving the active course.
- Map courses start from the current fleet position, including mid-flight. Select a discovered destination, then Engage/GO TO or double-click; remembered detours offer RESUME. Preserve pan/zoom across state updates.
- Map travel runs at fixed 8x with Pause and Play only; there are no selectable speeds or multiplier label. Travel advances only while Map is active and visible. Switching away freezes travel time, movement, fuel use, lab RP, income and passive travel repairs without changing manual/mandatory pause reasons. Returning resumes only an otherwise unpaused route. Continue retains its existing manual pause until Play/Engage. ETA uses 8x and researched cruise rate. Arrival does not force an informational or destination-choice modal or switch tabs. Battle speed controls are unchanged.
- Map shows position, heading, route/detour, destination distance/fuel/ETA, access restrictions and known station services. No large fuel-range circle, region tint/shaded bar or automatic reachability overlay.
- Docked services live in existing Map/Fleet side panels. Actual declared shipyard/refuel/trade capabilities determine access; Stop, pause, proximity or a stale legacy node never grant services. Unbeaten arrival encounters remain available through their contextual action.
- Encounters open frozen deployment in the existing battle scene. Arrange after seeing the mission and visible opponents, then press the top-center Begin Combat button. Combat settles once and returns to the same expedition position/route. Continue during a pending battle returns to deployment with the last saved placement; this is not a mid-fight restore.
- Combat retains existing targeting, abilities, visibility, support effects, asteroids and Warden behavior. The current slice does not require seamless in-world battles or new boss phases.
- Battlefield radius is 2400 units (3x), with faction centers 2520 units apart (3x). Ships within squadrons use compact boxes, independently of the large distance between fleets. Deployment, corridors, reinforcement entry, Warden placement, camera framing and saved formations use the enlarged geometry. Existing pan/zoom supports both the full battlefield overview and close inspection.
- Both factions retain the enlarged weapon ranges and Warden lance reach. Basic gun/laser range is 660; Missile Rack base range is 1320 (2x), or 1650 with Guided Munitions. Bullets travel at 640 units/second; missile speed remains 200 with existing guidance. Support/ability radii are unchanged.
- Asteroid densities Clear/Sparse/Medium/Dense provide 0/16/32/48 roots in zero to three compact fields between the fleets. Each field drifts coherently at roughly 2-5 units/second with slight individual variation. Clear locations stay clear; seeded layouts freeze into encounters and survive Continue. Existing collision, cover, fracture, ore and 48-root/96-rock limits remain.

## Ships and loadouts (fleet-command D1)

- Equipment exists only on ships, with unique instance IDs. No equipment buying, selling, shared stock or drag-fitting.
- Buy Ships sells fully fitted ships. Named fits live in [ship_loadouts.json](../data/ship_loadouts.json); availability, lookup and fitted pricing are shared through [ship_loadouts.gd](../scripts/ship_loadouts.gd).
- Every predefined fit fills every mount, including New Run defaults. Most equipment is initially available: lasers, missiles, railguns, Lab (M), Lab (XL), cabins, support equipment, Tactical Computer. Only Beam Lance requires research. Flight Deck and Bomber Bay are retired. Hide locked fits and reject unavailable fits/parts before mutation.
- Labs exist only in M and XL sizes. Former L-lab mounts in predefined fits use Large Plate: +100 hull, mass 8, catalog price 160 credits. Large Plate uses the missing-art marker until supplied art is available. The science Survey fit retains its central cargo hold; Observatory retains its XL lab.
- New Run has Foundation only, with no forced research or exclusive branch. Previously installed locked advanced equipment remains inspectable/removable; Continue never grants copies or free research.
- Shipyard refits choose complete named loadouts and are free, even at zero credits or in debt. There are no custom part-fitting/removal controls. The right panel lists read-only equipment effects and carried loads alongside complete loadout choices. Replaced parts are scrapped. Occupied holds/cabins must deliver before a preset replaces them; compatible retained loads stay attached to their UID.
- Fitted purchase price is chassis plus half its parts' prices. Group purchases use the squadron default. Loaded-equipment guards and all-or-none persistence still apply.
- Exactly one flagship deploys; its destruction ends the run. Original chassis artwork and catalog tuning remain in [slot_chassis.json](../data/slot_chassis.json) and [slot_equipment.json](../data/slot_equipment.json).
- New runs start with 13 fully fitted ships: one selected flagship, ten Drones, one Fighter and one Transport. There is no fleet command-capacity mechanic or per-ship command cost; purchases depend on fitted price and actual shipyard access. Flagship identity is explicit chassis metadata. Compact escort wings fit the Fleet canvas, with station artwork behind ships. Continue preserves owned fleets rather than granting new escorts.

## Squadrons (fleet-command D3)

- Same-chassis groups own a stance, formation centroid, default fit and per-ship overrides. Grouping never duplicates equipment or rearranges ships.
- Seek & Destroy preserves normal automatic combat. Defend Flagship guards the flagship; Evade avoids threats and does not automatically fire. Explicit attack orders override passive stance behavior. Repair support remains available while ships follow these orders.
- Tactical placement belongs to battle deployment. Clicking a ship always selects only that ship; clicking its numbered flag selects the squadron. Drag the ship or flag during setup to move the individual or group; group movement preserves relative spacing and clamps every hull inside the deployment area. Shift adds ships/flags and box selection adds individual ships. Empty clicks deselect. White sprite-fitting highlights and pan/zoom remain. Flags follow both factions' visible living squadrons; friendly flags are selectable. Begin Combat atomically saves formation and squadron centroids before starting; failed saves leave setup and an explicit error.
- New-run Drones start in a compact 4/4/2 box ahead of the flagship, with 78-unit spacing and physical hull/steering clearance. Fighter and Transport sit separately beside the flagship. Saved tactical placements remain player-controlled. Fleet displays those same saved boxes and all subsequent formation edits. Alien role/tier/wave squadrons likewise deploy and reinforce in separate compact boxes; copied wingmates never share a spawn point.
- Squadron actions include default refit, Reset overrides, quoted Repair and Buy N. Default refits preserve overrides unless deliberately reset. Group changes/purchases/repairs commit atomically.
- Ships can split a member into a new group or join a compatible one without moving/refitting it. Membership/orders may change during travel, not during pending battle or after run end.
- Fleet ship selection shows individual stats, travel repair rate, complete loadout choices, fitted equipment effects and carried loads, paid repair, confirmed salvage and split/join in the right panel. Squadron selection shows aggregate stats, shared stance, default loadout, override reset, group repair, reinforcements and member selection there. Actual shipyard restrictions still apply; Ship inspection has no modal editor or separate fitting canvas; all details and settings stay in the right panel. Ship and flag dragging edit the same saved formation used by battle setup. Fleet saves on release, with preview rollback and an explicit error if saving fails.
- Battle friendly ship/flag selection replaces the right enemy list with the individual's loadout/equipment or squadron member list, current living count, hull/shields and stance. Setup includes drag guidance; combat selection is inspection only, with no refitting/repair/reassignment controls. The shared X clears selection and restores the enemy list. Live enemy clicks retain existing fleet attack targeting; live drags retain camera panning and never move deployment positions.
- Fleet and battle setup share individual formation positions and squadron centroids. Fleet uses the same relative hull scale and spacing, with its own preserved camera/zoom. Whole-group dragging clamps one displacement using every hull radius. Fleet uses the open deployment bounds; narrower mission lanes can constrain placement when entering battle setup. Changes made there save with Begin Combat and appear in Fleet/Continue. Battle setup advances no combat, support/ability cooldowns, waves, damage, ammunition, rewards or map time; ability and speed controls enable only after Begin Combat. Fog and visibility remain truthful.
- Battle reuses the main screen's authored command frame: allied squadrons left, enemy ships grouped by name right, active ability controls in the bottom panel, and time controls in the top bar. Side lists scroll, show surviving/deployed counts and aggregate orange hull/blue shield meters, and include combat reinforcements. Enemy rows respect current visibility. Lost ships contribute no health while original deployed capacity remains the denominator; zero allied hull still counts as alive. The frame consumes pointer input, camera framing uses the open central battlefield, and the frame hides for results. The footer remains visible without researched abilities. Friendly reports aggregate each deployed squadron's damage, deflection, losses and surviving repair HP, retaining a separate flagship group and explicit passenger casualty fees.
- The shared live combatant budget is 192 across initial deployments, enemy summons and frozen waves. Ordinary generated enemies now have two identical wingmates each (3x original counts, previously 2x), with fresh entity IDs and the same stats, role, tier and wave. This applies to campaign and expedition encounters; the verified opening deploys 15 enemies against 13 player ships. No separate 24-enemy balance cap applies; finite threat allocation and the shared safety ceiling bound generation. Boss bodies, pylons, framework organs/craft, rewards, hazards and boss timings are not replicated. Full live budgets defer/reject spawns without spending finite allowances.
- Both factions use deterministic squadron cohesion and velocity alignment before existing collision separation and obstacle avoidance. Stronger alignment keeps squadmates together through long-range flight arcs. Friendly membership follows owned squadrons; aliens group by role, tier and wave, including reinforcements. Different explicit orders/stances do not pull each other around; shield retreat, withdrawal and boss anchors take priority. Enemy readouts follow actual alien squadron membership.
- Distant idle guns retry searches at the existing 0.2-second AI cadence; nearby targets, explicit attack orders and interception bypass that delay. Fine spatial queries preserve the original separation neighbors/sum order and scout escort cover. Physical support/lab indexes invalidate on stat refresh; enemy steering keeps the same nearest eligible anchor. Conservative projectile bounds and per-step shape reuse retain exact silhouette contact tests.
- Battle retains 30Hz simulation with at most eight steps per callback and up to one simulated second of catch-up time after a costly frame; long stalls cannot create an unbounded backlog. Beam drawing avoids redundant segments when fog is disabled, subpixel exhaust uses fewer particles, and cached white shield masks preserve source alpha. The focused 1280x720 50-vs-40 check measured about 18ms median / 32ms p95 with the fixed 60Hz frame budget; actual wall-clock pacing at the 5x setting measured about 4.8-5.0x in the short fight. This is scoped evidence, not a universal 60fps or long-soak guarantee.
- Older physical-v2 saves derive groups without changing equipment, health, loads or formation. Invalid membership/defaults/anchors are rejected, not silently repaired.

## Trade, rewards and fleet ammunition

- Cargo/passengers follow equipment UIDs, not mount indices. Holds carry 10 goods; cabins carry four passengers. Trading-port departure loads eligible equipment; delivery requires a different trading port. Returning to origin does not cash out.
- Destroyed hosts lose their loads; passenger casualty fees apply once and may create debt. Deliver before removing occupied equipment.
- Event/battle rewards use committed receipts: credits, RP, fuel, missiles and occasional rescued ships. No equipment loot. Non-victory and duplicate settlement grant no rewards.
- The distress rescue grants a fully fitted Transport with fresh IDs and empty loads, including for a large fleet. It never substitutes credits for a command-capacity limit. Fuel recovery is tank-capped.
- Fleet missiles persist across encounters/withdrawals. Each Missile Rack projectile spends one; partial final salvos are allowed and empty racks stop. Enemy weapons and finite carrier payloads do not draw from this pool. Magazines/computers never create missiles.
- The battle footer's MISSILES ON/OFF button controls new allied missile launches fleet-wide, including before Begin Combat and while paused. Other weapons and enemy missiles are unaffected. Existing rounds continue flying and rack cooldowns keep counting down while OFF; switching ON never resets a cooldown or banks extra clusters. Fire control defaults ON each encounter/Continue restart and is recorded in battle commands for deterministic replay.

## Research and repair

- Research earns from traveled distance: installed living Lab (M) and Lab (XL) produce 1 and 3 RP per travel day, with existing chassis bonuses and fractional progress retained. No fleet baseline; stopped/docked/event/battle time does not generate travel RP.
- Research uses one shared bar: the first choice costs 20 RP, then the threshold increases by 20 RP per chosen upgrade. Each fill offers three unique, seeded eligible upgrades; the Research Flagship adds one option while retaining its +25% installed-lab output. Selecting a card grants it immediately and carries excess RP forward, including additional funded choices. Dependencies, story gates and exclusive branches remain backend eligibility rules. Fewer eligible upgrades produce fewer cards; a depleted pool banks earnings without filler.
- Equipment-specific offers require living deployed matching capabilities; future equipment variants declare the same metadata used by effects. Inventory and destroyed hosts do not qualify; zero hull remains alive. Removing the last matching item preserves a saved card but disables Choose with a concise requirement until a matching item returns, without RP debit or reroll.
- The tree screen and project focus are removed. A card dialog opens over the current management view when a saved offer is ready, waits for existing windows, and pauses travel while visible. Each card has a full name, one gameplay sentence and Choose; first-time branch commitments disclose their lock consequence. Shared X defers the same cards, resumes the previous travel state and leaves the header available to reopen them. The header shows exact whole RP/threshold, a bar and daily output. Closing/reopening or Continue never rerolls a pending offer.
- Card choices, RP debits, grants, score and resources remain atomic. Readiness is saved before a travel-earned offer opens; failed saves roll back the whole travel frame or choice and expose a retryable error. Selecting a card grants it immediately, without a separate research-complete banner. Research descriptions retain one short gameplay sentence and shared 11px styling.
- Fleet Logistics I/II/III and their command-capacity modifier have been removed from research, offers and descriptions. Remaining research retains 20/+20 RP progression, dependencies, equipment matching and science's fourth option. Purchases, rescues, deployment and checkpoint validation impose no command budget.
- Applied Energy adds 15% laser damage. Optics I adds 20% laser range and unlocks Beam Lance fits. Guided Munitions adds 25% guided-missile range. Research Methods I/II give operating labs +20%/+40% total output, multiplying host bonuses; the second tier replaces the first.
- Carrier I/II are removed; 18 upgrade cards remain. Renewal I adds 25% passive hull-repair rate and Repair Drone Hangar healing rate. Command I raises each uplink contribution to 30% damage amplification; Command II accelerates computer synchronization by 25%. The empty Command/Covert commitment is removed. Only Beam Lance research grants equipment access; no research grants physical items.
- Prismatic Lasers turns Laser/Double Laser into sustained beams that ramp on uninterrupted contact; range/contact loss, target changes or obstruction reset the lock.
- Siege I splits player missile projectiles into finite interceptable fragments; only the parent spends ammunition. Siege II adds 20% fragment damage and railgun damage reaching hull, preserving shield absorption and baseline penetration. Bastion I periodically restores shields with a visible pulse; it cannot exceed capacity or revive a host.
- Field Engineering increases cruise speed by 25% and reduces fuel use per distance by 25%. Map trip and underway ETAs use the same researched rate. It does not accelerate the simulation clock, repair, combat or idle time; total journey distance, lab output, income and authored encounter positions stay unchanged. Civil Hospitality pays four credits/travel day at cumulative quarter-day milestones crossed while owned, never retroactively.
- Travel effects derive from chosen research on Continue and apply only after selection, without retroactive earnings or travel changes. Field Engineering's short detail/toast sentence is "Cruise faster using less fuel." Encounter reduction is deferred until random encounters exist; authored/story events are never suppressed by this upgrade.
- Travel income uses actual distance, not idle time. Wallet updates during cruise must not rebuild views or reset selection/pan/zoom. Save failure rolls back credits, score, fuel, distance, research, repair and deliveries together.
- Expedition time advances only while cruising on the map. Docked, stopped, paused and encounter-blocked fleets do not accumulate time or passive hull repair. Living hull self-repairs during moving expedition time; combat repair effects and paid shipyard repair remain unchanged.
- Command Uplink marks last five seconds with a 20-second cycle and range 500. Each operational uplink adds 20% allied damage (30% with Command I); copies on one host and allied marks on the same target add together. Source loss/expiry removes contributions. Each Shield Relay adds 10 shields to its host and transfers up to two existing shields per second within range 180. Copies add capacity and transfer rate. Relay shields recharge normally at 4%/second (0.4/s per copy) after four seconds without damage. An empty donor cannot transfer until it recovers; recipients need their own shield capacity. Transfers stop at equal shield fractions without creating extra shields.
- Actual relay transfers show blue particles flowing from donor to recipient, stopping when no shields transfer. Alien shield hits use the same sprite-alpha glow as player ships; Sentinel cover uses a subtle silhouette glow instead of a large circular halo. Support mechanics and source artwork are unchanged.
- Ion Emitter hits for 8 before its existing shield/hull multipliers (8 shield DPS / 2 hull DPS). Missile Rack fires a cluster of five 12-damage missiles every eight seconds: 60 burst damage, 7.5 nominal DPS and one fleet missile per parent. Distinct launch positions and warm particle exhaust make the guided rounds visible; interception, partial final clusters and splitting research remain. Beam Lance hits for 48 every four seconds with its existing 1.2-second aimed charge, which can miss movers. Mounts, prices and ranges remain. Focused comparisons: [EQUIPMENT_REVIEW.md](EQUIPMENT_REVIEW.md), [BATTLE_FEEL_AUDIT.md](BATTLE_FEEL_AUDIT.md).
- Active support remains ship-wide: magazines add +20% cycling per copy; Tactical Computers charge a host-wide weapon cooldown reset every 12 seconds with one copy, six seconds with two; Command II makes one copy charge in 9.6 seconds. A ready reset waits for a visible unshielded enemy in weapon range. It preserves an ongoing beam charge and uses normal ammunition; it does not grant free shots. Railguns and Command Uplink remain active.
- Disruption I grants Ion Pulse to living ships with an installed Ion Emitter. Target a visible enemy within emitter range to interrupt its charge and suppress shield recovery for four seconds; all emitters share a 24-second cooldown. The existing ability bar supports manual targeting, optional Auto and Pause Aim; source selection appears only when there is a choice. Pause freezes cooldowns and battle speed advances them with simulation time. There is no material or credit cost.
- Ion Pulse cooldowns/effects and aim preferences are encounter-local: Continue restarts them with the saved encounter, not mid-fight. Earned research survives Continue; new runs still start without abilities. This does not enable full fog or deferred Scan/mining/gravity abilities.
- Repair Drone Hangar continuously heals surviving self/allied hull within range 220. Each hangar contributes 2 hull/s total, shared equally among damaged recipients with unused shares redistributed; multiple hangars add output. Renewal I raises each to 2.5 hull/s. Green particles show actual healing. There are no launched repair craft, payloads or 20-hull allowance; destroyed ships never heal and lost loads never return.
- Flight Deck/Bomber Bay and Carrier I/II are removed from active equipment, fits and offers; the Siege/Carrier branch commitment is removed. Civic Repair Escort keeps its repair hangars; Frigate Command Escort uses a Command Uplink in the former deck mount. Drone remains the cheapest chassis at 150 credits and one command point, with three general S mounts and one general M mount.
- Extraction, slot bonuses, Gravitic Snare/Disruption II, ECM/Phase and component-rebuilding effects remain deferred; preserve old earned/installed data without offering unsupported new grants or inventing replacements.

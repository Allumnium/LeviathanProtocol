# Hangar stock and drag fitting

The physical hangar no longer uses equipment dropdowns on ship mounts. The right panel shows uninstalled, owned equipment instances. Ship selection and repair/salvage actions remain on the left; the ship and its live stats occupy the center.

Mount labels are aligned into two columns beside the ship, with connector lines to actual mounts. At rest, only occupied mounts show a card and connector; empty cards and their lines appear only during equipment drags, showing slot size/type. Each fitted card is one line with the full equipment name. There are no extra headings, Empty lines, abbreviations or wrapping. Rail widths and card positions remain reserved even when empty cards are hidden, keeping ship scale and drop geometry stable before, during and after dragging. Side cards are display-only: they cannot select equipment, start drags, accept drops or trigger preview snapping. All fitting/selection/transfer gestures use the actual mounts in the ship art; start unequipping there and drop into the right panel. No text or opaque controls cover chassis/module art.

Mouse-wheel zoom is centered on the pointer (0.4x-4x); hold the middle mouse button to pan. Only the ship, fitted equipment and actual mount targets move/scale. Side cards stay fixed/readable, their connectors track the transformed mounts, and deterministic stars remain stationary and clickable. Camera position/zoom is retained per ship across refits, repairs, purchases and tab changes during the current screen session, without adding save fields. Wheel input over stock remains stock scrolling; covered fitting views do not intercept shop input. Floating equipment uses the current zoomed mount size, including while zooming during an active drag, and snapped previews use the same transformed bounds as placement.

Fitting camera verification: 531 drag checks pass both headless and native, including pointer-anchored zoom, middle-button pan, zoom limits, fixed stars/cards, per-ship camera restoration, shop/stock input isolation, zooming during a drag and three-resolution floating-art scale. All 1,326 mount checks and 51 native fleet/map polish checks pass.

- Stock and equipment buying share independent checkboxes for Offense, Defense, Tech, Civic and S/M/L/XL. All start checked; multiple choices in each group are combined, and the category and size groups intersect. Clearing either group shows no matches. Choices stay synchronized between the visible panels and survive purchases, refits and ship selection within the screen.
- No search or ordering controls. Stock and equipment/chassis purchases always run from lowest to highest catalog credit price; stock ties use instance ID. Stock damage still affects sale proceeds, not list order. Chassis buying has no filters.
- Drag a stock row onto a compatible mount. Matching mounts highlight during the drag; an invalid target cannot fit the item.
- Drag installed equipment between compatible mounts, or back onto the stock panel to unequip it. Replacing an installed item returns that instance to stock.
- While installed equipment is being dragged, the entire right panel becomes the unequip target, including its frame/padding. Stock controls temporarily hide and "Drop here to unequip" is centered in their place. Release/cancellation restores the same controls and filter preferences. Loose-stock drags leave the stock UI visible. There is no permanent unequip hint in the list.
- Dropping elsewhere cancels without changing ownership. Loaded passenger/cargo equipment must deliver before it can move. Deferred/locked equipment cannot be fitted.
- Each loose equipment card has a coin icon at the right of row two. Click once to show Sell? with cancel and checkmark; the checkmark occupies the coin's exact position. Cancel restores the summary. Confirm sells that physical UID for its existing condition-adjusted value. Only one confirmation is active; filtering cancels it. Failed saves retain the item/credits and permit retry/cancel. There is no Manage panel or separate Stock modal. Ship repairs remain directly available beside the roster.

With no part selected, integrity (current/max), shields, mounted-weapon DPS, cargo space and passenger space occupy the bottom area. Selecting an installed part directly on the ship replaces all ship statistics with the part's full name, current/max HP, mount size/type and relevant effect/rate. Blank-space clicks, empty mounts and unequipping the selected part restore ship statistics. The bottom region reserves a constant height so these switches do not move or rescale the ship or its drop targets. Cargo/passenger capacity counts operational installed equipment, not stock or destroyed hardware. Capacity constants are shared with travel/trade rules. DPS retains the existing direct mounted-weapon definition; finite carrier sorties are not represented as sustained DPS. Selecting installed equipment shows its name and HP inline; damaged/destroyed mounts use shared-theme colors.

`hangar_equipment_transfer.gd` checks drag ownership/run identity, research, size/position and loaded-equipment restrictions. Final changes go through the existing atomic `GameState.fit_equipment` transaction. No inventory clone, credit charge or alternate save format is introduced. A failed save leaves the live loadout unchanged.

The stock renderer computes research grants once per refresh. Drag highlighting shares grants across mount checks. Mounts, stock rows and filter checkboxes do not participate in Tab/arrow focus cycling. Starhull, shared theme styling, no hover tooltips and shared window X controls are preserved.

## Equipment sprites

The owner's mapped `assets/equipment/cargo_xl_d.png` is assigned to the existing XL Cargo Hold through the optional `art` field in `data/slot_equipment.json`. Stock rows show a small thumbnail; the floating drag sprite uses fitted scale from the current ship, not a fixed-size thumbnail. Installed drags use their source mount; loose stock uses the first compatible mount on the displayed ship. Hovering a compatible mount snaps the preview to that mount's exact bounds before release, using the same fitted-art calculation and circular-base anchor metadata as the installed sprite. Its floating text hides while previewing a sprite target. After a valid drop, it renders in the mapped slot, preserving aspect ratio and transparency. The fitted art replaces the opaque text placeholder, and its entire slot remains a drag target. Equipment without art keeps its full name in the side card and uses an outlined drag preview.

The same cached mounted-art rendering is used in formation, map/report previews and battles. Textures are resolved when equipment units rebuild, not repeatedly loaded during drawing. Damaged/destroyed equipment retains the shared health colors. Art is presentation metadata: equipment IDs, XL fitting rules, cargo capacity, ownership, repairs and save format are unchanged. Original source art is unmodified.

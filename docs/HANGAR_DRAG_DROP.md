# Hangar stock and drag fitting

> **Superseded.** Drag-and-drop fitting and the shared equipment stock were removed by the fleet-command D1 loadouts change. Ships are refitted from named loadouts or per-mount part choices at shipyards; see [HANGAR.md](HANGAR.md#ships-and-loadouts-fleet-command-d1). The retired tests are in `docs/history/pre_loadout_tests/`. This file is kept as history.


The physical hangar no longer uses equipment dropdowns on ship mounts. The right panel shows uninstalled, owned equipment instances. Ship selection and repair/salvage actions remain on the left; the ship and its live stats occupy the center.

Mount labels are aligned into two columns beside the ship, with connector lines to actual mounts. Only occupied mounts show a card and connector, including during equipment drags; empty mounts never show labels. Each fitted card is one line with the full equipment name. Connectors are consistently opaque white, one physical screen pixel wide, without antialiasing, with vertices snapped to pixel centers; equipment category, damage and drag validity do not recolor them. There are no extra headings, Empty lines, abbreviations or wrapping. Rail widths and card positions remain reserved even when empty cards are hidden, keeping ship scale and drop geometry stable before, during and after dragging. Side cards are display-only: they cannot select equipment, start drags, accept drops or trigger preview snapping. All fitting/selection/transfer gestures use the actual mounts in the ship art; start unequipping there and drop into the right panel. No text or opaque controls cover chassis/module art.

Mouse-wheel zoom is centered on the pointer (0.4x-4x); hold the middle mouse button to pan. Only the ship, fitted equipment and actual mount targets move/scale. Side cards stay fixed/readable, their connectors track the transformed mounts, and deterministic stars remain stationary and clickable. Camera position/zoom is retained per ship across refits, repairs, purchases and tab changes during the current screen session, without adding save fields. Wheel input over stock remains stock scrolling; covered fitting views do not intercept shop input. Floating equipment uses the current zoomed mount size, including while zooming during an active drag, and snapped previews use the same transformed bounds as placement.

Fitting camera verification: 531 drag checks pass both headless and native, including pointer-anchored zoom, middle-button pan, zoom limits, fixed stars/cards, per-ship camera restoration, shop/stock input isolation, zooming during a drag and three-resolution floating-art scale. All 1,326 mount checks and 51 native fleet/map polish checks pass.

- Stock and equipment buying share independent checkboxes for Offense, Defense, Tech and Civic, plus a two-handle inclusive S/M/L/XL size slider. Categories start checked and the size range starts S through XL. Both endpoints are included; handles may meet on a single size but do not cross. Category choices intersect the size range; clearing categories shows no matches. Choices stay synchronized between the visible panels and survive purchases, refits and ship selection within the screen.
- No search or ordering controls. Stock and equipment/chassis purchases always run from lowest to highest catalog credit price; stock ties use instance ID. Equipment has no damage/condition and sells for half its catalog credit price. Chassis buying has no filters.
- Drag a stock row onto a compatible mount. Matching mounts highlight during the drag; an invalid target cannot fit the item.
- Drag installed equipment between compatible mounts, or back onto the stock panel to unequip it. Replacing an installed item returns that instance to stock.
- While installed equipment is being dragged, the entire right panel becomes the unequip target, including its frame/padding. Stock controls temporarily hide and "Drop here to unequip" is centered in their place. Release/cancellation restores the same controls and filter preferences. Loose-stock drags leave the stock UI visible. There is no permanent unequip hint in the list.
- Dropping elsewhere cancels without changing ownership. Loaded passenger/cargo equipment must deliver before it can move. Deferred/locked equipment cannot be fitted.
- Each loose equipment card has a coin icon at the right of row two. Click once to show Sell? with cancel and checkmark; the checkmark occupies the coin's exact position. Cancel restores the size/category summary. Confirm sells that physical UID for half its catalog price. Only one confirmation is active; filtering cancels it. Failed saves retain the item/credits and permit retry/cancel. There is no Manage panel or separate Stock modal. Whole-ship repairs remain directly available beside the roster.

With no part selected, hull (current/max), maneuverability, mass and shields use the supplied comparison bars in the bottom area, alongside mounted-weapon DPS. Movement is consolidated into a chassis-average-weighted heuristic with average base score 50; descriptions show rounded integers without changing backend precision. Cargo and passenger space appear independently only when capacity is greater than zero. Selecting an installed part directly on the ship replaces all ship statistics with the part's full name, mount size/type and relevant effect/rate, without equipment HP. Blank-space clicks, empty mounts and unequipping the selected part restore ship statistics. The bottom region reserves a constant height so these switches do not move or rescale the ship or its drop targets. Cargo/passenger capacity counts all installed equipment on a living host, not stock; equipment never takes damage or switches off independently. Capacity constants are shared with travel/trade rules. DPS retains the existing direct mounted-weapon definition; finite carrier sorties are not represented as sustained DPS.

`hangar_equipment_transfer.gd` checks drag ownership/run identity, research, size/position and loaded-equipment restrictions. Final changes go through the existing atomic `GameState.fit_equipment` transaction. No inventory clone, credit charge or alternate save format is introduced. A failed save leaves the live loadout unchanged.

The stock renderer computes research grants once per refresh. Drag highlighting shares grants across mount checks. Mounts, stock rows and filter checkboxes do not participate in Tab/arrow focus cycling. Starhull, shared theme styling, no hover tooltips and shared window X controls are preserved.

## Equipment sprites

Ship details omit the shield bar when shield capacity is zero and omit DPS when
there are no mounted-weapon DPS. This applies equally to chassis previews, Fleet
details and the editor. Positive values remain integer-formatted; hiding them
does not change the reserved editor bottom height or fitting geometry.

The owner's mapped `assets/equipment/cargo_xl_d.png` is assigned to the existing XL Cargo Hold through the optional `art` field in `data/slot_equipment.json`. Stock rows show a small thumbnail; the floating drag sprite uses fitted scale from the current ship, not a fixed-size thumbnail. Installed drags use their source mount; loose stock uses the first compatible mount on the displayed ship. Hovering a compatible mount snaps the preview to that mount's exact bounds before release, using the same fitted-art calculation and circular-base anchor metadata as the installed sprite. Its floating text hides while previewing a sprite target. After a valid drop, it renders in the mapped slot, preserving aspect ratio and transparency. The fitted art replaces the opaque text placeholder, and its entire slot remains a drag target. Equipment without art uses the shared pink placeholder square everywhere fitted art appears (see Placeholder assets in HANGAR.md), and keeps its full name in the side card.

The same cached mounted-art rendering is used in formation, map/report previews and battles. Textures are resolved when equipment units rebuild, not repeatedly loaded during drawing. Equipment has no damage tint. Art is presentation metadata: equipment IDs, XL fitting rules, cargo capacity and ownership are unchanged. Original source art is unmodified.

The latest drag checks also verify pre-drop bounds and unchanged fitted size, full-panel unequipping and rejection of side-card drags/drops. `test_ui_polish.gd` additionally drives both size handles through native input, including coincident endpoints, and checks shop/stock synchronization, compact audio sliders and Research camera restoration.

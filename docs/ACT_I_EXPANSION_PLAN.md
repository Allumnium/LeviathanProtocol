# Equipment, events and Act I expansion plan

2026-11-05. The user subsequently approved implementation of this proposal. The equipment, presets, authored story/events and focused checks are delivered; see [current gameplay](HANGAR.md) and [completion results](../COMPLETED.md). Missing artwork remains an explicit dependency in [TODO.md](../TODO.md). The review findings and proposed decisions below record the pre-expansion design baseline, not the current catalog. XP and Ion Pulse balance remain separate decisions.

## Recommendation

Build a coherent civilian-to-conscription Act I around Port Meridian before increasing encounter volume. Add seven main story beats, eight authored side-event briefs and four equipment candidates in two small batches. Reuse the existing map, stations, deployment, trade, research, rewards and Warden battle. Every new part must change a complete loadout's role and have an observable runtime effect.

The narrative basis is [STORY_SPEC.md, Act I and narrative rules](STORY_SPEC.md). The gameplay contract is [HANGAR.md](HANGAR.md), with [EQUIPMENT_REVIEW.md](EQUIPMENT_REVIEW.md), [BATTLE_FEEL_AUDIT.md](BATTLE_FEEL_AUDIT.md), [RESEARCH_REDESIGN.md](RESEARCH_REDESIGN.md) and [SHIP_EXPERIENCE.md](SHIP_EXPERIENCE.md) supplying the relevant decisions and proposals. Where older passages conflict, use the latest implemented behavior and explicit contract. No history-folder document was used to derive requirements.

| Finding | Evidence and implication |

See [current gameplay](HANGAR.md). |
| Equipment variety exceeds what the player can actually select. | The [equipment catalog](../data/slot_equipment.json) has 23 obtainable size/rank entries after the runtime retired/deferred filters; Beam Lance is additionally research-gated. The [24 active presets](../data/ship_loadouts.json) do not mount Ion Emitter, Beam Lance, Ammunition Magazine or Tactical Computer. Under complete-loadout fitting, those existing parts need usable presets as much as new hardware does. The separate 36-preset proposal covers some of this gap, but not Ion Emitter or Ammunition Magazine. |
| The opening contradicts the narrative's first-contact order. | Of [12 authored event records](../data/expedition_events.json), `first_contact` puts aliens near the initial route at [165, -24]; `drone_vanguard` fires at 420 cumulative distance. Derelict, distress and nebula text also reveals aliens before any Meridian incident. The story explicitly reserves direct alien contact for the refueling-station attack. |
| Meridian is currently a name, not the story's turning point. | [Locations](../data/expedition_locations.json) contain Kessler Refuel Post and a naval Meridian Outpost, but no station-departure attack, return/depart choice or remembered station outcome. |
| Distance currently substitutes for story causality. | The Protocol fires at 700 cumulative travel units and grants 150 credits plus gate access. Detours or repeated travel can reach that distance without completing a narrative sequence. Gates need causal flags; distance can still pace optional encounters. |
| Event support is deliberately small. | [ExpeditionEvents](../scripts/expedition_events.gd) supports start/distance/zone/arrival, one positive `requires_flag`, up to four choices, simple rewards, flags and combat. Its detour validator currently accepts only POIs, not a return to a station. |
| Choice resolution and battle success are different transactions. | [GameState](../scripts/hangar_state.gd) records an event choice when battle is admitted; settlement grants rewards on victory, and a non-victory arrival battle can be retried. | More battles are not automatically more variety. | Existing encounter generation uses [alien rosters and templates](../scripts/encounter_generator.gd). Calling a patrol template “pirates” would still spawn aliens. Pre-Meridian human conflict should be noncombat in this tranche; actual human enemy fleets would be a separate extension. |
| Progression needs a measured target. | The bounded Defensive opening audit recorded 7.568/20 RP after its first win. That is historical evidence for one route, not a prediction for the expanded act. Free refits also mean a part's price only affects fitted purchases; it cannot balance a universally superior refit. |

Current documentation also contains stale statements: HANGAR still says 18 cards and one Drone command point despite the removal of Logistics/capacity (15 offered cards remain), and its Ion Pulse range sentence conflicts with the 300-range finding in BATTLE_FEEL_AUDIT. Reconcile these against runtime when their respective slices are touched; do not treat them as permission to change balance. Station `stock` metadata even mentions retired Flight Deck, but is not a reason to restore equipment stock or carriers.

## Equipment proposal

First expose useful existing hardware through the same preset review: reserve a legal Ion Emitter fit and a magazine-supported missile fit, and retain the proposed Beam Lance/computer roles. Those are loadout-content changes, not new equipment. Replace or revise candidate fits in [the existing progression proposal](loadout_progression_proposal.json); do not silently grow beyond one/two starter choices and four total per chassis. Leave XP rates, rank assignments and rookie rules in their existing review track.

All numbers below are first comparison values, not approved catalog changes. Ordinary new equipment needs no new research unlock; existing capability matching remains explicit. Use exact size/category compatibility and fully populate every mount.

| Candidate | Proposed effect and cost | Role, tradeoff and compatible host examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Autocannon — M, Offense** | Four ordinary kinetic projectiles, 4 damage each, every 2 seconds; range 480, mass 8, catalog price 100. No missile-pool debit, splash or extra targeting rule. Nominal 8 DPS before accuracy/modifiers. | Close-range direct fire versus Double Laser's 7.2 DPS at range 660 and mass 6. Compatible with Frigate `center`, Drone `center` and Combat flagship outer M mounts. It gives up reach, mobility and laser research scaling; compare actual projectile performance before adoption. |
| **Light Shield — S, Defense** | +6 host shields, mass 3, price 40; normal recovery rules, `shield` research capability. No transfer aura. | Gives small hulls a personal shield at the expense of a gun, plate or thruster. Fighter `fore` is compatible; its side mounts are Offense only. Defensive flagship front/rear S mounts also accept it. Retain the M generator's larger capacity; test mass and slot tradeoffs against Single Plate. |
| **Cargo Pod — M, Civic** | Capacity 4 goods, mass 4, price 85; existing cargo purchase/delivery values and UID ownership. No passive money generation. | A commercial Frigate or Drone can carry a small load by giving up its central weapon/armor. Existing XL hold keeps capacity 10. On science/civic flagships, the pod competes with labs, cabins or shields. |
| **Passenger Berth — XL, Civic** | Capacity 12 passengers, mass 12, price 250; existing per-passenger fare and casualty fee. No premium fare multiplier. | A Transport passenger fit exchanges its XL cargo hold or lab for civilian transport. Civic flagship central XL also qualifies. More revenue means more passengers at risk; no automatic free rescue boarding. Ordinary port loading remains the source of carried passengers. |

Deliver the two combat parts first, then civic capacity variants. These fill gaps in the current size/role combinations without new ammunition, fabrication, morale, power, heat or component-health systems. No ECM/Phase, carrier restoration, L labs, Gravitic Snare, nanoforge or dormant equipment activation is implied.

Implementation requirements:

- Define real weapon/shield behavior in the shared catalog/runtime and concise equipment details. Autocannon must not inherit laser/ion/missile effects accidentally. Light Shield participates in existing shield recovery/Bastion/relay-recipient rules. Preserve magazine and Tactical Computer cycling/ammunition rules.
- [HangarTrade](../scripts/hangar_trade.gd) currently recognizes only two exact equipment IDs and fixed capacities of 10/4. Add explicit load-kind/capacity metadata with shared consumers for loading, totals, validation, delivery, casualty settlement and UI. This is required runtime work, not a JSON-only addition. Keep the existing hold/cabin capacities and prices unchanged.
- Define exact complete presets and prices, validate mounts, and identify which existing/proposed option each replaces. A new part with no selectable fit is not a delivered feature. Keep a legal ordinary starting/purchase fit for every chassis and avoid a new starter fleet redesign.
- Preserve equipment UIDs and occupied-load refit guards. Never move loads by slot, resize occupied equipment to discard contents, revive destroyed hosts or manufacture passengers/missiles through refitting. Zero hull remains alive.
- Obtain matching art for each new part. Until supplied/approved, retain the intentional missing-art marker and full item names/effects; do not borrow unrelated assets or read active resources from the archive.

## Act I main sequence

Use the existing first refueling stop (`refuel_station_1`, currently Kessler at [240, -35]) as **Port Meridian**. Rename the later naval `meridian_outpost` at [665, -125] to **Bastion Anchorage**. This places the civilian station before the current 420-distance escalation and avoids two competing Meridian identities. These are proposed narrative changes, not current names.

Keep Haven, Frontier Settlement, side POIs and Bastion Gate. Port Meridian becomes the first charter destination through existing map guidance; players may detour, but cannot advance the main story by accumulating distance elsewhere. Use concise destination/context feedback if they head for the locked gate. Do not add an arrival information modal or force camera movement.

| Beat | Trigger and player action | Persisted consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Convoy charter** | Revise the existing start briefing: ordinary commercial work, Port Meridian as the destination. Acknowledge and choose a route. | Retain the selected flagship/company identity in later dialogue using existing chassis identity. Combat, Civic, Research and general-purpose starts all remain valid; no extra company-class system. |
| **2. An ordinary port** | First Meridian arrival, before any attack. Short dockside exchange grounded in trade/refuel and civilian traffic, with normal services available. | Record `meridian_visited`. Establish a recognizable controller/civilian voice that returns in the distress message. Do not grant a mandatory refit or assume the player bought fuel. |
| **3. Contact at the docking ring** | First actual departure after that visit, once the route starts moving. An unidentified organism attaches; station control calls for help. | Record `meridian_incident`; choose **Return to defend** or **Continue course**. Show that returning starts combat and that leaving offers no rescue reward. Preserve the outbound destination for later Resume. No direct alien encounter can precede this beat. |
| **4. Meridian defense — optional** | Returning reaches the station and enters frozen deployment against a small existing alien patrol. The encounter represents clearing the approach while the station handles evacuation. | Victory records `meridian_saved` and pays a bounded reward once. Living withdrawal records `meridian_withdrawn`, with no victory reward; leaving records `meridian_left`. Flagship loss still ends the run. No new station-health objective or boss phase. |
| **5. The station report** | One short aftermath appropriate to saved/left/withdrawn, before the next main beat. | Successful defense preserves the port; the other outcomes report evacuation failure and the abandoned port closes services. Keep other refueling ports reachable. No morality meter, hidden ending lock or repeated attempts to farm the rescue. |
| **6. Leviathan Protocol** | After the incident's outcome is resolved, admit the mandatory broadcast at the next safe event boundary. Both branches converge here. | Retain the existing 150-credit requisition and `leviathan_protocol` gate access, paid once. Explain capital-vessel conscription and the order to clear Bastion Gate en route to assembly. Preserve company identity in the response, not a choice that pretends to cancel conscription. |
| **7. The blocked assembly route** | Travel via the naval anchorage if desired, then the existing Bastion Warden battle. | Retain Warden mechanics, rewards, score and Demo Complete. Closing text establishes passage toward the combined fleet. The Warden is a gate-blocking organism, not the original Leviathan or the Act II branch-defining confrontation. |

This preserves the playable demo finale while aligning Act I's dramatic endpoint with conscription. Actual military assembly, the first Leviathan battle, infestation, the chrysalis revelation and endings remain future acts. Foreshadowing must not explain the stolen child or identify a morally correct faction.

## Eight side-event briefs

These are authored one-use encounters placed on routes/at existing locations, not a random-event pool. Rework existing events where appropriate instead of adding eight interruptions on top of all twelve old records. A normal route should see a small subset; optional detours expose more. Each choice needs a concrete consequence or new information, with no more than four options.

| Brief / timing | Choice and consequence | Reuse or required work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fuel on the margin — frontier** | Buy 40 fuel for 60 credits or decline; show actual tank-limited gain. The trader mentions unusually tight corporate inspections. | Rewrite existing `free_trader`; preserve affordability and tank cap. No alien claim before Meridian. |
| **Missing survey vessel — frontier** | Follow a beacon to the derelict field or stay on course. Find an incomplete navigation record and ordinary salvage, not alien bodies. | Rework `derelict_signal`/early salvage. Retain detour/Resume, one-use resource rewards and no equipment loot. |
| **Sealed corporate convoy — frontier** | Accept a paid fuel transfer or refuse; obtain a restricted-route clue either way. Proposed comparison: give 10 fuel for 25 credits. | Add an explicit minimum-fuel requirement: current negative fuel effects clamp at zero and would otherwise permit underpayment. No physical mystery cargo or theft revelation. |
| **Patrol inspection — frontier** | Submit records or pay a disclosed expedited-clearance fee; the compliant option is free. Dialogue distinguishes military concern from corporate secrecy. | Existing credits/flag effects suffice. Do not simulate idle waiting as productive travel or add human combat. |
| **Settlement shortage — frontier** | Spend a quoted amount on local relief or decline; later testimony remembers the choice. Normal cargo delivery remains handled by trade. | One-use choice flag and a later text variant. No invented quest cargo, duplicate delivery payment or reputation system. |
| **Hauler under attack — after incident** | Defend for the existing fitted Transport rescue, or leave. | Reuse `distress_call` and `relay_defense`; gate behind `meridian_incident`. Reward only on victory with fresh IDs and empty loads; retain current non-story encounter retry rules. |
| **Filaments in the nebula — after incident** | Take the existing +10 RP / -10% maximum-hull study option or leave. A living fitted lab provides a short additional observation, with no bonus RP in this pass. | Reuse `nebula_study`; capability-conditioned text is the only new affordance. All fleets can choose the baseline study. Keep actual hull consequences visible and no certainty about alien motives. |
| **Requisition queue — after Protocol** | Donate a disclosed credit amount to civilian relief or retain funds for the fleet; dialogue acknowledges Meridian's outcome. | A short anchorage event using existing credits and story conditions. A practical, conflicted officer replaces a caricatured villain. No ship seizure, forced loadout or mandatory payment. |

Reassign `first_contact` to Meridian's defense role. Gate/reposition `drone_vanguard`, `swarm_scouts` and alien-bearing corridor/salvage variants after the incident; avoid stacking them into an immediate series of mandatory fights. Keep optional asteroid risk/reward where it serves route choice. Do not roll new encounters on repeated travel, and leave Field Engineering's random-encounter reduction deferred.

## Minimal story plumbing and persistence

1. Extend validated authored conditions with explicit required/excluded flags and narrowly scoped option requirements where needed (fuel and living equipped capabilities). Keep conditions declarative; reject malformed or unknown references. Validate that every mandatory beat has a reachable no-cost choice.
2. Add a one-shot departure boundary tied to a visited station and actual movement. Changing the selected course, pausing, switching to Fleet, failed saves or reopening Continue must not trigger the incident twice. Freeze travel while its choice is open. Extend the existing detour/Resume path only enough to return to a station.
3. Commit story flags and receipts with the existing atomic choice/battle transaction. Freeze outcome effects into the pending encounter; apply saved/withdrawn outcomes only at settlement. Distinguish the Meridian branch's terminal withdrawal from ordinary retriable arrival encounters. Exactly one aftermath must become eligible; a surviving player must always be able to reach the broadcast.
4. Use expedition flags as the authority for these story outcomes. Existing research `story_flags` serve different gates; do not create an unsynchronized second copy of Meridian state. Keep the current Warden research/completion behavior explicit.
5. Make service availability derive from station metadata plus Meridian's outcome. UI, docking/trade, refuel and refit transactions must share that predicate; a stale station panel cannot offer services at an abandoned port. Preserve actual location checks. Validate fuel reachability of Settlement/Anchorage on both branches before finalizing placement/rewards.
6. Admit ready story follow-ups at a deterministic boundary, ahead of optional distance events but after the current battle/report/modal closes. Never advance time or accumulate lab RP/repairs while resolving dialogue. Do not let already-passed distance thresholds dump a queue of stale early encounters after a flag changes.
7. Use concise event titles, one short scene paragraph, explicit costs/risks and brief result text. Replace the current generic “New destination revealed” feedback for flags that merely record a choice. Reuse the event window, shared X policy and existing result areas; preserve pan/zoom and no hover/focus navigation.

There is no request for a generic quest engine, procedural world, tow/rescue simulation, new save architecture or save migration. Preserve current run ownership and transaction guarantees while implementing the new authored content.

## Decisions before implementation

- Adopt the proposed first-stop Port Meridian mapping and keep the Warden as the demo epilogue to conscription. If Meridian must remain at the later naval location, relocate every pre-incident alien encounter and remeasure the longer civilian opening instead.
- Select or revise the four candidates and their comparison values; agree the exact preset substitutions within the four-per-chassis cap. Existing Ion Emitter/magazine access should be resolved in the same review.
- Set the desired first research-choice milestone and the acceptable cost/reward budgets for Meridian and optional aid. Keep Ion Pulse reach and XP balance as their existing separate decisions.
- Approve final event copy and matching artwork before claiming content/presentation complete. Missing art can remain an explicit dependency, not a reason to remove names or substitute unrelated images.

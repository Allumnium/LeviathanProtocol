# Tactical visibility — step 36

New departures use shared team sight and radar. Each surviving Command Center provides sight 260 and radar 480, in battle-world units, centered on its actual component position and rotation. Enemy hulls have the same base source; craft, summons, decoys and organs do not independently supply a Command Center. Both teams use the same query and the union of their living sources. Advanced sensors and relays remain optional and unavailable.

`BattleVisibility` supplies current sight, coarse explored terrain memory, anonymous radar contacts and five-second last-seen markers. Radar payloads contain only kind/position; stale payloads add age and keep the last legitimately observed position. Neither contains entity IDs, HP, equipment or authoritative actor references. Destroying a source immediately removes its coverage. Explored memory remains after current coverage is lost. Foundation Scan extends coverage temporarily through the same service.

Targeting and AI use these rules. Visual contact permits precise attacks; radar permits investigation of an anonymous position, and stale information permits visiting the old point. Without a contact, ships follow a deterministic search route rather than reading hidden enemy positions. Friendly escort anchors remain known. Losing sight drops automatic and commanded locks. Projectiles retain their last legitimate aim point and radius, stop reserving hidden-target damage, and expire on a miss; they can still physically hit something at their retained aim point. A new sighting does not resurrect a lost lock automatically.

The renderer filters ships, neutral actors, drops, projectiles, explosions and beam segments. Unexplored space is dark; explored areas remain dim. Generic radar rings and stale crosses differ in shape as well as color. A compact radar view uses the same observations. Automatic and finishing cameras cannot follow hidden enemies, and battle reports omit current hidden enemy details. No hover tooltips, source labels, HP readouts or hidden loadouts are attached to generic contacts.

Visibility mode is frozen in each departure's shared context. Older pending deployments without that field retain their previous behavior so their recorded battle inputs remain reproducible; new attempts use current rules. Detached legacy movement/combat fixtures can still omit the field. Explored/stale state is deterministic in authoritative replay snapshots; this does not add exact midbattle resume beyond the existing interrupted-deployment policy.

`tests/test_visibility.gd` covers both factions, shared sources and source destruction, radar sanitization, lock removal, immutable last-known positions and expiry, explored memory, ballistic loss of guidance, hidden reports, frozen mode validation and replay at several batch sizes. Native rendering verifies pixel-for-pixel that changing an unseen on-screen actor changes no image pixels, including radar; camera framing also ignores hidden motion. A 64-entity refresh measured approximately 0.38 ms on the current host. Evidence: `.local/step36/visibility-final.log` and `docs/ui-review/visibility.png`.

The opening fleets remain subject to real component losses. A destroyed cargo hold repaired after arrival remains empty; the opening regression now accepts that legitimate paid reconstruction instead of requiring every seed to carry goods by the pre-boss stop. Economy and opening checks are rerun with visibility and Scan as part of the final baseline.

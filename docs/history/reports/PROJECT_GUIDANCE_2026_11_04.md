# Trade and research

Passenger/cargo loads follow equipment instance IDs, never mount indices. Holds carry 10 goods; cabins carry 4 passengers. Cargo loads when leaving an inhabited port; cabins board for inhabited destinations. Delivery pays credits automatically at inhabited arrivals. No Port/Story buttons or load-management modals. Occupied items must deliver before being unequipped. Destroyed hosts/components lose their loads; passenger casualty fees apply once and may leave credit debt. Repairs never recreate lost loads.

Research is one pannable tree with major branches and minimal inline details/Set Focus. No category filters, Foundation node, View Grants modal, disclosure glyphs or next labels. Completed nodes have a green check above the name; partial nodes show X / Y. Only the header shows unspent RP. Lab I / II / III produce 1 / 2 / 3 RP/day respectively, with no fleet baseline. Multiply by travel days (typically 2-4), freeze at departure, retain fractions and settle once on arrival. Battle duration never generates RP. Research progress is not shop currency.

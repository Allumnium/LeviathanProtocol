# Active controls and Foundation Scan — steps 38–39

Step 45 follow-up: live asteroids, persistent finite ore and mining Auto now use Scan's discovery records. Foundation Scan is the first active ability. It comes from an operational Command Center, needs no lab, reveals radius 180 at a ground point within 450 of that component's actual rotated position, lasts six battle-simulation seconds and has a fleet-wide 25-second cooldown. It adds no damage modifier or research points. Research still uses completed travel days; these ability durations are battle seconds.

The shared themed bottom bar supports six pinned actions and click-open overflow. Only implemented, authorized actions appear in production; other abilities remain gated. Scan has Auto, an automatic or explicit source choice, range/area previews, and optional pause while aiming. Clicking the action enters targeting; clicking ground casts, and Escape/right-click cancels. Targeting freezes camera movement and preserves the prior pause state. Feedback and cooldowns appear inline. Controls use the shared Starhull theme and disabled keyboard focus navigation, without tooltips or new windows.

Auto investigates radar contacts and scans unknown visible rocks using only observed positions and its own discovery records. It never reads an unseen actor's contents or position to choose a cast. Both teams obey the same range and source rules. Scan records rock contents without recording permanent live coordinates; knowledge remains after temporary sight expires. This discovery API is tested on detached asteroid fixtures; live asteroid actors and durable finite ore remain steps 41–43. Advanced spectroscopy overlays and cloak/decoys remain optional.

Cooldowns are shared per fleet/type, independent of which ship supplied the cast. Destroying a source cancels its active reveal without refunding the cooldown. Buying, repairing or changing a source cannot reset a cooldown. Remaining player cooldowns and Auto/pause preferences are additive run metadata; older saves default safely. New departures freeze the initial ability state and authorized IDs for replay, while live/retry setup uses the latest persisted remaining cooldown. Menus and quiet travel do not advance the battle clock. Interrupted battle recovery can conservatively restore an earlier, larger remaining cooldown, never a free cast.

Production casts and preference changes commit before their live effect. A failed write reveals nothing and spends no cooldown, resources or RP. Save rejection is recorded explicitly in command envelopes so a replay does not need a failing filesystem to reproduce it. Auto retries failed persistence at most once per simulation second; a manual retry remains available. Fixed-tick command IDs prevent duplicate activation, and effects, discovery knowledge, cooldowns and rejection handling are included in replay diagnostics.

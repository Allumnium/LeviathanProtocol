# Economy UI and loaded revisions — October 29, 2026

Step 33 adds held cargo's credit value beside signed spendable credits in the header. Cargo value is informational; construction, repairs and research do not spend it. The header retains its single RP balance and level, with no duplicate fleet-condition or Act/row text.

Fleet Summary shows deployed passenger occupancy and operational places, cargo quantity/capacity, and estimated income. A single selected hull shows its own occupancy, current luxury range, cargo quantity/capacity and income beside the ship. Estimates use surviving operational cabins at their current luxury plus the cargo loads' frozen origin values. With a planned route the label is Arrival, and uninhabited destinations show zero. With no route selected, Next port shows a conditional estimate for the next inhabited stop. These estimates assume the relevant components/loads survive travel; they never mutate funds or loads.

Offload and Disembark are small actions beside the selected hull. Offload sells only that physical ship's goods at the stored value, without refill. Disembark safely releases only that ship's passengers at a secured port, without fees, refunds or tickets. The previous fleet-wide disembark backend API remains available for compatibility. The contextual actions remain within the formation viewport and choose a position that avoids covering the selected hull when space allows; there are no new tooltips or service windows.

Loaded hulls remain protected from reserve, salvage and family-revision changes. All affected physical copies must be empty before changing a family's active revision; this neither mutates old hulls nor grants free replacements. Unloading commits before live mutation. Failed writes preserve funds, physical loads, occupancy and ledger. Repeated actions, reopening the port and reload do not sell the same goods twice or board/refill the same arrival again. During deployment, disembark/offload are rejected.

Changed implementation: `game_state.gd` (reviewed starting credits, pure fleet/ship economy summary and scoped disembark), `game_map.tscn`/`game_map.gd` (held value), `fleet_panel.gd` (summary and contextual values/actions), and `formation_board.gd` (context placement). Shared Starhull/theme inheritance and pointer-only controls remain in use. No optional freight/sensor/hazard feature was activated.

`tests/test_economy_ui.gd` constructs two mixed cabin/cargo ships with starting resources and uses actual inhabited arrivals. It verifies fleet/scoped estimates, zero income for uninhabited routes, negative credits distinct from cargo value, failed saves, mouse-driven per-hull unloading, multi-copy revision guards, repeated port actions, exact proceeds and reload. Native checks cover 640×360, 1280×720 and 1920×1080, header fit, contextual panel fit and selected-hull visibility. Existing passenger-loss UI assertions now use the selected-ship action. The baseline runner includes this suite and the new opening-loop playtest; the battle suite's new-run budget expectations are updated to 300.
